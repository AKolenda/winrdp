A small client.
A proper remote desktop.

Connect directly over your LAN or VPN. Your session goes to your Windows computer, with no Win RDP account or relay in between.

One window per computer

Keep saved computers close. Open separate session windows, resize your desktop, or switch to full screen with the connection bar within reach.

UDP v1, v2, and v3. Powered by Rust.

Our IronRDP fork implements experimental reliable UDP transport with automatic TCP fallback. UDP v2 is the default and has been tested against live Windows hosts. UDP v3 (RDP-UDP2) still needs live-host validation.

Explore the IronRDP fork

Choose what you share

Per-computer controls for clipboard, remote audio, microphone, and printer redirection. Availability depends on your desktop and the Windows host’s settings.
